About TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a modern, lightweight productivity application designed to help you manage your daily tasks efficiently. Built entirely with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, it requires no backend or database — all your data is stored securely in your browser's Local Storage.

Whether you're tracking work assignments, study goals, or personal errands, TaskFlow keeps everything organized and accessible — even without an internet connection.
